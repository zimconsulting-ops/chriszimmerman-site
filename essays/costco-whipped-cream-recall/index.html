Costco whipped cream recalled in NC before Thanksgiving

Some cans of Kirkland Signature Real Heavy Whipped Cream may contain a small amount of a cleaning chemical. Here is how to check yours.

Christian Zimmerman · November 13, 2026

If you bought whipped cream at Costco between October 11 and October 31, check the can before Thanksgiving. Alamance Foods has recalled one batch of Kirkland Signature Real Heavy Whipped Cream, the 15-ounce can, item number 1777000, because it may contain a small amount of a cleaning chemical, after members reported a chemical smell and taste.

How to tell if yours is affected

Look for a best-by date of APR/02/27 printed on the can along with a timestamp between 00:35 and 02:45. Alamance Foods said in its November 7 notice that no serious adverse effects requiring medical care had been reported.

What to do

Stop using it and take it back to Costco for a full refund. Questions go to Alamance Foods at customer.care@alamancefood.com.

The recall covers North Carolina and eleven other states: Alabama, Florida, Georgia, Iowa, Illinois, Indiana, Missouri, Mississippi, South Carolina, Tennessee and Wisconsin.

Sources: Alamance Foods recall notice to Costco members (Nov 7), WRAL (Nov 12).
